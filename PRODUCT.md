# Product: Performance Cleaning Services website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (GitHub Pages), Cal Sans from Google Fonts, no frameworks, no tracking
- **Register:** brand (Persuade mode: a one-page site for a local service business)

## Users
- People in and around Newark, NJ who want a home deep-cleaned, an office or commercial space cleaned, or a newly built or renovated space cleaned after the trades leave.
- Contractors and builders who need a job site cleaned for handover (inferred: the owner posts in "New Jersey Construction Connection").
- They arrive from a Facebook post or group ad, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook ad into a phone call or a written request for a free estimate. Secondarily, give the owner a real web presence to hand out (they have none: no site, no Maps listing, no reviews).

## Positioning
A cleaning crew that does the heavy jobs: deep cleans, commercial spaces and post-construction cleanup. Post-construction is the differentiator against residential-only cleaners (Muse note).

## Not known (never invent)
Email, owner name, hours, years in business, towns served, prices, reviews, work photos (only one video thumbnail of a dirty tub, no after).

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone call (confirmed). Text: unconfirmed, so every text path is paired with the call number.
- No email: none in the footer until the owner supplies one.
- Phone-first; must load fast on cellular in Facebook's in-app browser.

## Brand commitments
- Black-and-gold logo → gold carried as the accent; page ground is navy and paper, never black.
- Voice: "we", warm and plain, a little playful.

## Principles
1. The phone's first screen says who, what, where and how to reach them.
2. Only their own facts. No invented reviews, prices, years or towns.
3. Calm band style (house default) coloured from the logo.
